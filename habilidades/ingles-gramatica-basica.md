- Para atender, basta o essencial: presente simples, "can", "will" e perguntas curtas. Não tente falar difícil.
- Verbo to be: I am, you are, he/she is, we are, they are. Pergunta: "Are you safe?" Negativa: "I am not sure."
- Presente simples: "I work here." "She lives in Bonfim." Na 3ª pessoa leva s: "He needs help." Pergunta com do/does: "Do you have a photo ID?" "Does he go to school?"
- Futuro e pedido: "I will call you." "Can you come on Monday?" "Could you repeat, please?" "Please bring your documents."
- Perguntas abertas: what, where, when, who, how. "What do you need?" "Where do you live?" "How many people live in your home?"
- Passado simples para o relato: "What happened?" "When did it start?" Verbos comuns irregulares: go/went, have/had, take/took, come/came.
- Dica: frase curta, sem gíria, sem phrasal verb quando der ("ask" em vez de "reach out"). Fale devagar e repita o essencial.
